# Gran Turismo (Sony / Polyphony Digital): long-term fun and revenue, as of November 2026

Research note on method: GTPlanet and the PlayStation Blog returned HTTP 403/404 to direct fetches, so findings attributed to them come from search-result extracts of those pages, not a full read. Items fully read are Push Square (Dec 2025), Final Weapon (Oct 2026 patch notes) and Instant Gaming (Nov 2026). "Verified" below means stated by Sony, Polyphony Digital or Kazunori Yamauchi; "estimate" means a third-party tracker or leak.

## 1. Which design pillars drive long-term fun?

### Takeaway
GT7's retention rests on a collection-driven "car life" loop (earn credits, buy, tune, collect, photograph) wrapped in guided goals (Café menu books, licences, Collector Level) and refreshed by time-limited stock, weekly events and ranked online racing. The structure gives every player type a reason to return: collectors, tuners, hot-lappers, photographers and competitive racers.

### Cited Findings
- The GT Café sits at the centre of the world map and guides players through car-collection "Menu Books"; 39 menus at launch, each with cars to collect, events and rewards. Car designers and engineers appear to explain a car's history. — [racinggames.gg](https://racinggames.gg/gran-turismo/gran-turismo-7s-new-gt-cafe-is-a-celebration-of-car-culture/)
- The Café has kept growing: by update 1.72 (October 2026) Extra Menus reach No. 57 (High-Performance EVs, Tuned GT-Rs, Le Mans Winners), gated at Collector Level 70+, 80+ and 90+, and a new Seasonal Menu asks players to collect different cars each season for extra rewards. — [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/10/15/gran-turismo-7-update-1-72-spec-iv-part-1-patch-notes/)
- Collector Level (the collection-based progression meter) cap was raised in Spec III (Dec 2025) and again to 90 in update 1.72; engine swaps unlock at Collector Level 50, and 10 more swap-eligible cars were added in 1.72. — [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/10/15/gran-turismo-7-update-1-72-spec-iv-part-1-patch-notes/); [gran-turismo.com Spec III](https://www.gran-turismo.com/gb/news/00_4185758.html)
- Rotating scarcity: new cars are split between Brand Central (always available) and the Used Cars dealer (rotating stock); Legend Cars prices are revised periodically using Hagerty valuations (next revision scheduled March 2027). — [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/10/15/gran-turismo-7-update-1-72-spec-iv-part-1-patch-notes/)
- Car culture presentation is still being extended four and a half years in: 1.72 added 9 brands to Brand Central (including Lancia, TVR, Shelby, Pontiac), new Jeep and TVR museum sections, new designer conversations triggered by owning specific cars, a Garage "Car Viewer" for photography, and two new featured Scapes curations. — [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/10/15/gran-turismo-7-update-1-72-spec-iv-part-1-patch-notes/)
- Licence and mastery content was expanded after launch: Spec II (Nov 2023) added 50 new challenges/licence-style tests (Master Licence) alongside a snow track. — [Traxion](https://traxion.gg/gran-turismo-7-spec-ii-includes-sophy-2-0-ai-50-new-licence-tests-and-snow-track); [Push Square](https://pushsquare.com/news/2023/11/biggest-gran-turismo-7-update-ever-adds-cars-tracks-modes-challenges)
- Weekly Challenges (introduced with Spec II, Nov 2023): a set of five events that must be completed within the week; completing all five has paid a 6-Star Roulette Ticket (Car) or, in other weeks, a 500,000 Cr ticket. Still running in July 2026. — [GTPlanet, Nov 2023](https://www.gtplanet.net/gran-turismo-7-weekly-challenges-20231108); [GTPlanet, Jul 2026](https://www.gtplanet.net/gran-turismo-7-weekly-challenges-yin-and-yangwang-20260702/)
- Sport mode: Daily Races are ranked events with skill-based matchmaking. Driver Rating (DR, speed) runs E to S (seven grades per the manual extract); Sportsmanship Rating (SR, clean driving) starts at B; DR cannot exceed SR, so dirty driving caps competitive rank. Qualifying is done as an untimed time trial outside the race session. — [GT7 online manual](https://gran-turismo.com/gb/gt7/manual/sportmode/02); [Coach Dave Academy](https://coachdaveacademy.com/tutorials/gt7-daily-races-explained/)
- Physics is treated as a living system: 1.72 changed default suspension and differential settings for Gr.1 to Gr.4 cars; Spec III added a Data Logger and a Dunlop tyre partnership; in an August 2026 evo interview Yamauchi discussed tyre physics as a problem still unsolved. — [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/10/15/gran-turismo-7-update-1-72-spec-iv-part-1-patch-notes/); [gran-turismo.com Spec III](https://www.gran-turismo.com/gb/news/00_4185758.html); [boxthislap (secondary summary of evo interview)](https://boxthislap.org/kazunori-yamauchi-gran-turismo-tire-physics/)
- Nostalgia is used deliberately: the 1.72 title screen was redesigned in the style of the first Gran Turismo (1997), and classic fictional tracks return over time (Grand Valley in 2023, Autumn Ring planned December 2026). — [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/10/15/gran-turismo-7-update-1-72-spec-iv-part-1-patch-notes/); [TheSixthAxis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/?p=437810)

### Inferences
- The loop works because it layers several goal horizons: per-race (payout), per-week (Weekly Challenges, Daily Races rotation), per-update (new cars and menus), and open-ended (Collector Level, gold medals, DR/SR). A player always has a short goal and a long one.
- Scarcity and rotation (used and legend dealers, invitations) convert a static car list into a reason to log in and to hold a credit reserve, which is also what makes credits worth buying.
- Collection gates on content (Extra Menus at Collector Level 70 to 90) turn the back catalogue into endgame content at almost no production cost.
- DR capped by SR is a cheap, legible rule that aligns competitive ambition with clean behaviour.

### Gaps
- No source retrieved for the Daily Workout mechanic (drive roughly a marathon distance per day for a roulette ticket); it is widely known but not confirmed here.
- No verified current totals for cars and tracks in GT7 as of November 2026 (launch and current counts not confirmed by a retrieved source).
- No data on which mode holds the most play time (single-player vs Sport mode vs Scapes).

## 2. How has GT7 been supported since March 2022, and what does engagement look like?

### Takeaway
GT7 (released 4 March 2022) has had free content updates roughly every one to two months for more than four and a half years, punctuated by large "Spec" updates (Spec II Nov 2023, Spec III Dec 2025, Spec IV Oct and Dec 2026). Engagement is rising rather than decaying: Yamauchi stated over 2 million monthly active users in December 2025, with new users increasing.

### Inferences
- The cadence is small-and-regular (3 to 5 cars every 6 to 8 weeks) plus one annual "event" update that resets attention. The Spec II doubling claim suggests the tentpole updates, not the monthly drips, are what move active-user numbers.
- 2026 updates increasingly add systems that reduce friction (Car Concierge, unlimited invitations) rather than only content, which reads as a response to long-standing grind complaints.
- No GT8 has been announced as of the sources found; Polyphony is treating GT7 as a platform, now in its fifth year.

### Gaps
- No daily active user figure, retention curve or play-time data has been published.
- The share of GT7 players using PS VR2 is not public.

## 3. How does it make money?

### Takeaway
Gran Turismo is PlayStation's best-selling exclusive franchise: over 100 million units as of 25 June 2025 (verified). GT7 revenue is premium-price sales first, with optional credit packs, one paid DLC (Power Pack, $29.99, December 2025) and a web of manufacturer, tyre, wheel and esports partnerships on top. Sony has never published GT7 unit sales; the best current figure is a third-party estimate of about 12.7 million copies and $840 million (November 2026).

### Inferences
- Dividing the Alinea estimates gives roughly $66 revenue per copy ($840M / 12.7M). That is above a typical discounted average selling price for a $60 to $70 game, which suggests the estimate includes credit packs, deluxe editions and DLC, but Alinea's method is not disclosed, so this is a rough reading only.
- The identical "12.7 million" figure appears for both GT Sport (leak) and GT7 (Alinea estimate). This may be coincidence, but it is a reason for caution when quoting the GT7 number.
- If the franchise moved from 90M+ (Dec 2022) to 100M (June 2025), about 10 million units were added in roughly 2.5 years, almost all of it GT7, consistent with a GT7 total in the low-to-mid teens of millions.
- The business model evolved: premium sale plus credits (2022), then four years of free updates as retention and marketing, then a paid single-player expansion aimed at the most engaged players (2025). The paid DLC arrived only after trust had been rebuilt and after the audience was at its largest.
- Partners pay for or co-fund presence because the game is a showroom: wheel makers get native support and a "brand" page, carmakers get concept cars and esports teams. This is revenue and content at once.

### Gaps
- No official GT7 unit sales, revenue or microtransaction revenue from Sony; Sony earnings material does not break out the title.
- Sponsorship and licensing fee values are not public.
- Could not verify system-seller effects (console attach rate, PS VR2 or wheel sales uplift) with numbers.
- GT5, GT Sport (official) and GT6 per-title figures were not confirmed from an official list in this pass (a 2.37 million GT6 figure appears in secondary coverage; GT Sport's 12.7 million is leak-sourced).
- The full text of Yamauchi's 25 March 2022 blog post could not be fetched; specific figures often quoted from it (reward increases of about 100%, wallet cap raised to 100 million Cr) are not confirmed by retrieved sources.

## 4. What does Yamauchi say about design philosophy and retention, and what are the common criticisms?

### Takeaway
Yamauchi frames Gran Turismo as a vehicle for preserving and spreading car culture ("car life simulator"), not just a racing game, and has publicly credited sustained updates for GT7's unusual late growth. The persistent criticisms are economy-related: slow credit earning relative to top car prices, random roulette rewards, time-gated purchase invitations, always-online single-player, and expensive credit packs.

### Cited Findings
- Yamauchi says he feels a responsibility to keep car culture alive into the future and wants to bring as many people as possible close to what he loves; collecting cars is itself "another crucial side to car culture"; he calls the car one of the most beautiful industrial products. — [GameSpot](https://gamespot.com/articles/gran-turismo-7-wants-you-to-love-car-culture-again/1100-6500238/); [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/kazunori-yamauchi-gran-turismo-7-interview/)
- Pre-launch he positioned GT7 as a return to the classic GT structure (campaign, tuning, used cars) after the esports-focused GT Sport. — [GTPlanet](https://www.gtplanet.net/?p=108162)
- On late-life growth (Dec 2025): GT7's status "is probably the best of any Gran Turismo titles that we've had in the past" and "We've never experienced this phenomenon before, and neither has PlayStation." — [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2025/12/sonys-never-experienced-this-phenomenon-before-ps5-smash-hit-gran-turismo-7-getting-stronger-over-time)
- On PS VR2 (Feb 2023): GT7 was built for it from day one; he called the result "historically significant". — [GTPlanet](https://gtplanet.net/gt7-built-for-psvr2-from-day1-20230205)

### Inferences
- The core driving and presentation were strong enough to survive a severe trust crisis; the economy was the only part of the game that generated lasting hostility, and Polyphony spent four years loosening it (higher payouts, selling cars, weekly credit tickets, unlimited invitations, Car Concierge).
- Random reward wheels with visibly poor odds generate resentment out of proportion to their economic weight. Deterministic rewards (Weekly Challenge tickets with stated values) were better received.
- Yamauchi's "culture first" framing gives non-racing features (museum, Scapes, designer interviews) a clear purpose and differentiates the series from pure competitive sims.

### Gaps
- No full interview transcripts were read; quotes are from press summaries.
- No recent (2026) Famitsu interview was located.
- Community sentiment in late 2026 (GTPlanet forums, Reddit) was not sampled directly; the criticism evidence is weighted to 2022.

## 5. Which lessons transfer to a small independent drag racing game?

### Takeaway
The transferable parts are structural, not budgetary: a start-cheap, earn, buy, tune loop; guided collection goals; medal tiers on short skill tests; rotating stock; a small regular update rhythm with an occasional larger "spec" update; a fair economy with deterministic rewards; and a clean-racing rating if online play exists. The parts that do not transfer are licensed-manufacturer breadth, photoreal presentation and esports events.

### Cited Findings
- Guided collection works as onboarding and as endgame: Café menus started at 39 and reached No. 57 by October 2026, with later menus gated by Collector Level. — [racinggames.gg](https://racinggames.gg/gran-turismo/gran-turismo-7s-new-gt-cafe-is-a-celebration-of-car-culture/); [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/10/15/gran-turismo-7-update-1-72-spec-iv-part-1-patch-notes/)
- A weekly five-event set with a fixed completion reward has run continuously since November 2023. — [GTPlanet](https://www.gtplanet.net/gran-turismo-7-weekly-challenges-20231108)
- Small regular drops (3 to 5 cars per update in 2026) sustain a 2M+ monthly audience; the large Spec II update is the one credited with doubling active users. — [GTPlanet](https://gtplanet.net/gt7-really-high-active-users-kazunori-yamauchi-20231213); [gran-turismo.com](https://www.gran-turismo.com/gb/news/00_8895693.html)
- Cutting payouts after launch while selling currency triggered a record-low user score and forced an apology and reversal within about a week. — [KitGuru](https://www.kitguru.net/gaming/matthew-wilson/gran-turismo-7-now-has-the-lowest-user-score-of-any-playstation-game-ever/); [The Drive](https://www.thedrive.com/news/44911/gran-turismo-7-is-sorry-will-give-1m-free-credits-and-make-serious-changes)
- Paid content was introduced only after years of free updates, and was aimed at challenge and structure for engaged players, bundling currency as part of the value. — [GTPlanet](https://www.gtplanet.net/gran-turismo-7-power-pack-first-impressions-20251204/)
- Engine swaps and tuning options keep being added as progression-gated depth (unlock at Collector Level 50). — [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/10/15/gran-turismo-7-update-1-72-spec-iv-part-1-patch-notes/)
- DR/SR: a speed rating capped by a sportsmanship rating. — [GT7 online manual](https://gran-turismo.com/gb/gt7/manual/sportmode/02)
- Always-online single-player was a top complaint and a real outage risk. — [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/gran-turismo-7-outage-problems-live-service-microtransactions-reviews/)

### Inferences
- Start small and slow: give the player a weak first car and make the first upgrades feel large. Drag racing suits this well because the result is a single number (elapsed time) that visibly drops with each part bought.
- Use medal tiers on short tests: bronze, silver and gold targets on reaction time, launch, shift timing and bracket consistency are the drag equivalent of licence tests, cheap to author and highly replayable.
- Give collecting a spine: themed "menu" sets (for example three 1960s muscle cars, three sleepers, three imports) with a small story blurb and a reward provide direction without a large campaign.
- Rotate stock: a used-car lot or parts counter that changes daily or weekly creates a reason to check in and to save currency. Keep prices and odds visible.
- Prefer deterministic rewards to random wheels. If randomness is used, publish the odds and make the floor reward feel worthwhile.
- Never reduce payouts after launch; if the economy is too generous, add new sinks (new cars, cosmetic parts) rather than cutting income.
- Ship updates on a rhythm the team can keep: one or two cars or parts plus one event set on a fixed schedule beats irregular large drops, with one named, larger update per year to bring lapsed players back.
- Keep single-player fully offline; a small studio cannot afford the server risk or the goodwill cost.
- If monetising beyond the base price, follow the Power Pack pattern: a late, optional pack of structured events for committed players, not currency sold against a slow grind.
- Treat cars as culture: short history notes, a photo or showroom mode and nostalgia touches cost little relative to their effect on attachment.
- If online racing exists, add a simple clean-racing rating early; drag racing equivalents are red-light and lane-violation rates.
- Tuning depth is the mastery layer: gearing, tyre pressure, launch RPM and weight transfer are a drag game's version of GT's physics mastery, and a telemetry or data-logger view (as GT7 added in 2025) helps players learn from runs.

### Gaps
- No evidence was gathered on drag-racing-specific content inside Gran Turismo or on how GT players respond to drag events, so these lessons are extrapolated from circuit racing.
- Nothing here quantifies how much each feature contributes to retention; Polyphony has published no per-feature engagement data.
