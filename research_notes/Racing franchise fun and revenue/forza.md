# Forza franchise (Forza Horizon / Forza Motorsport): long-term fun and revenue, as of November 2026

Reading notes for the report writer:
- "Official" = stated by Microsoft, Playground Games, Turn 10 or shown by an in-game counter. "Estimate" = third-party analyst (mostly Alinea Analytics) or press calculation. Microsoft does not publish unit sales for Forza, so every sales and revenue figure below is an estimate unless marked official.
- "Players" always includes Game Pass subscribers; it is not copies sold.
- Several pages could not be opened in full (HTTP 403 or truncated), so some findings rest on search-result summaries of the article rather than the full text. These are marked "(summary only)".
- Latest data found: Forza Horizon 6 launch-window figures (May 2026), a July 2026 economy update, and the PS5 date announcement (8 October 2026). No Horizon 6 player or sales milestone later than about ten days after launch was found.

## 1. Which design pillars drive long-term fun in Forza Horizon?

### Takeaway
Horizon's retention rests on a low-friction open world plus a weekly drip of small, new goals (the Festival Playlist), and Playground itself ties this design to Game Pass: small, regular content and social hooks bring lapsed players back. Forza Horizon 6 (May 2026) kept the playlist but deliberately slowed the reward flow (rarer wheelspins, a seven-step wristband ladder) and promised that missed playlist cars will return, which amounts to the studio accepting the two main criticisms of Horizon 4 and 5.

### Cited Findings

- Mike Brown (creative director, Forza Horizon 5) said the Festival Playlist is where the live team keeps adding new content, races and cars for as long as the game is alive, and that seasons were a requirement because they had become so important in Horizon 4 (summary only, 2021 interviews) — [MSPoweruser](https://mspoweruser.com/forza-horizon-5-interview-with-mike-brown/); [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/seasons-return-in-forza-horizon-5-with-one-important-twist)
- The playlist was described in a 2021 Game Pass interview as "a snackable and manageable amount of stuff that's new", with "in a few days, there'll be some more", which "really helps blend with the way people play games on Game Pass" (attribution to Brown is implied but not explicit in the article) — [Press Start](https://press-start.com.au/news/xbox/2021/10/11/forza-horizon-5-was-built-entirely-with-xbox-game-pass-in-mind/)
- Horizon 4 received about five years of support before delisting, which shows how long the playlist model kept a single release alive — [Windows Central](https://windowscentral.com/gaming/xbox/xboxs-forza-horizon-4-is-set-to-disappear-from-digital-storefronts-after-five-years-of-support-but-you-still-have-time-to-buy)

User-generated content
- Horizon 5 kept expanding EventLab years after launch: the September 2023 update added a 2 km x 2 km sandbox "EventLab Island" with a larger prop budget and new prop packs — [Forza Support release notes, 12 Sept 2023](https://support.forza.net/hc/en-us/articles/20574248120595-FH5-Release-Notes-September-12th-2023)

Online, co-op and social
- Brown on why social features matter: "When someone in your friendship group plays the title, then all of your friends that are on Game Pass have that title," and games with features that prompt "Hey, can we do this thing in co-op?" do better on Game Pass — [Press Start](https://press-start.com.au/news/xbox/2021/10/11/forza-horizon-5-was-built-entirely-with-xbox-game-pass-in-mind/)

Accessibility and breadth of audience
- Horizon 5's PS5 audience skews mainstream: 89% also played GTA 5, 87% Fortnite, 72% Rocket League; only 24% played Gran Turismo 7 (Alinea estimate, January 2026) — [Alinea Analytics](https://alineaanalytics.substack.com/p/forza-horizon-5-races-past-5m-copies)
- Ralph Fulton (Playground) is reported as framing "fun" as the franchise's defining value (summary only) — [TheGamer interview](https://www.thegamer.com/interview-playground-games-forza-horizon-5/)

### Inferences
- The weekly playlist works because each instalment is small enough to finish in one or two sittings; "snackable and manageable" is the stated design goal, not just volume of content.
- Playground's own changes in Horizon 6 are the strongest evidence on reward pacing: after two games of very generous rewards, the studio chose scarcity and a visible ladder. That suggests generous rewards helped onboarding and breadth but weakened the sense of earning cars.
- Horizon's audience is mostly casual players who also play GTA, Fortnite and sports games, so approachability matters more than simulation depth for its scale.
- Player economies need active policing: both Horizon 5 (2022) and Horizon 6 (2026) had exploit-driven interventions.

## 2. How does Forza Motorsport differ, why was the 2023 game received worse, and what does that show about progression design?

### Takeaway
Forza Motorsport (October 2023) tied upgrade parts to per-car levels, forcing hours of grinding in each car before it could be modified; players rejected this, Turn 10 reversed it in stages, and the game ended active development after two years. Critics scored it in the mid-80s, but Steam users rated it "Mostly Negative", and its Steam launch peak was about 6% of Horizon 5's at the time.

### Cited Findings
- Complaint at launch: players had to grind for hours with each specific car to unlock upgrade parts; Turn 10 called it a "divisive topic" and said it was not delivering the upgrade experience many players expected — [VGC](https://videogameschronicle.com/news/turn-10-announces-changes-to-forza-motorsports-progression-system); [The Drive](https://www.thedrive.com/news/forza-motorsport-walks-back-controversial-car-upgrade-system-after-backlash.md)
- About a week after launch (October 2023) Turn 10 halved the time needed to unlock car parts — [GTPlanet](https://gtplanet.net/forza-motorsports-car-leveling-upgrade-20231017)
- Update 6 (March 2024) made all car parts available at Car Level 1 for every car; Turn 10 estimated two to three hours to reach Car Level 50 (pre-release coverage) — [Top Gear](https://www.topgear.com/car-news/gaming/forza-motorsports-latest-update-changes-how-car-progression-works); [VGC](https://videogameschronicle.com/news/turn-10-announces-changes-to-forza-motorsports-progression-system)
- Turn 10 named its "top three" complaint areas as car progression, race regulations (penalties) and AI behaviour — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/turn-10-promises-big-changes-to-address-top-three-forza-motorsport-complaints-rides-rules-and-racers)
- Some players felt the fix was a half-measure (forum opinion, not official) — [Forza forums](https://forums.forza.net/t/analysis-on-why-the-car-leveling-and-carxp-system-is-driving-people-away-from-forza-motorsport/653361?page=4)
- Critic scores: Metascore 85 (Xbox Series) and 84 (PC). Player scores: Metacritic user score 7.8 on Xbox but 3.9 on PC; Steam reviews fell to "Mostly Negative", with many citing performance and crashes — [Game World Observer](https://gameworldobserver.com/2023/10/11/forza-motorsport-launch-peak-ccu-steam-mixed-reviews); [Charlie Intel](https://charlieintel.com/forza/forza-motorsport-steam-reviews-plummet-amid-performance-issues-dont-buy-this-trash-281048)
- Steam launch peak was 4,703 concurrent players, against 81,096 for Horizon 5 in the same period (SteamDB, October 2023) — [Game World Observer](https://gameworldobserver.com/2023/10/11/forza-motorsport-launch-peak-ccu-steam-mixed-reviews)
- A report said most Forza Motorsport players had not completed a single online race (headline only; figure not retrieved) — [RacingGames.gg](https://racinggames.gg/article/most-forza-motorsport-players-havent-completed-a-single-online-race)
- Forum critics said the game lacked enough single-player content to hold players, leaving it dependent on an active online population (anecdotal) — [Forza forums](https://forums.forza.net/t/the-lack-of-online-players-is-very-concerning-and-not-enough-single-player-events/694026?page=4)
- Microsoft cut roughly half of Turn 10's staff in early July 2025 (over 70 people, per The Verge as cited); a former employee claimed the Motorsport side was shut and the studio would support Horizon; Microsoft did not confirm that at the time — [Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/xbox/forza-motorsports-future-doesnt-look-good-following-the-grim-week-at-xbox); [MP1st](https://mp1st.com/news/forza-motorsport-division-closed-studio-focuses-on-forza-horizon)
- December 2025: Turn 10 said "we do not plan to introduce new cars, tracks, features, or regular bug fixes for Forza Motorsport." Servers stay online, special events continue, and previously released Featured Tours and reward cars return monthly — [TheSixthAxis, 17 Dec 2025](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2025/12/17/turn-off-the-engine-and-retire-the-car-turn-10-signals-end-of-forza-motorsport-support/)
- Post-launch work before that included the progression rework, the Road Atlanta circuit and multi-class racing — [TheSixthAxis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2025/12/17/turn-off-the-engine-and-retire-the-car-turn-10-signals-end-of-forza-motorsport-support/)

### Inferences
- Gating a core pleasure (modifying a car) behind repetitive per-car grinding is the clearest progression lesson: players accept earning cars but resent re-earning basic functions for every car.
- Horizon gives players many things to do in any car at any time; Motorsport made the player commit hours to one car before it became interesting. The contrast is between rewards that widen choice and gates that narrow it.
- Motorsport's problems were compounded, not caused, by progression: PC performance, penalties, AI and thin single-player content all appear in complaints, so the failure should not be pinned on car levelling alone.
- The re-running of old Featured Tours and reward cars after development ended shows time-limited content can be recycled cheaply to keep a game alive.

### Gaps
- No official player totals for Forza Motorsport (2023) were found; Xbox and Game Pass numbers are not public, and Steam is a partial view.
- Details of Motorsport's multiplayer driver and safety ratings and how they affected retention were not retrieved.
- Whether a new Forza Motorsport is in development as of November 2026 is unknown; Phil Spencer's 2025 remark that the series was not dead ([OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/news/22674/no-forza-motorsport-isnt-canceled-after-massive-turn-10-layoffs-phil-spencer-confirms)) predates the December 2025 end of support.

## 3. How does the franchise make money?

### Takeaway
Forza Horizon earns from three layers: Game Pass reach (about 23 million of Horizon 5's 53 million players, per Alinea), full-price and premium-edition sales with paid early access (Horizon 6 Premium is $120; the Game Pass upgrade is $60), and late sales on other platforms (Horizon 5 on PS5: about 5.1 million copies and over $300 million, estimated). Horizon 6 reached 6 million players in its first week (official) and an estimated 4.9 million copies sold by 22 May 2026.

### Inferences
- A rough check on the unverified early-access revenue claim: 1.4 million early-access players at $60 to $120 each implies roughly $84 million to $168 million gross, so the reported $140 million is plausible but not confirmed.
- The 53 million / 23 million split implies around 30 million Horizon 5 players came from purchases or other access, but players are not copies, so this is not a sales figure.
- The business pattern is sequential: subscription reach first, premium upsell at launch, deep discounts later, then a new platform once costs are recovered. Each stage reaches a more price-sensitive audience.
- Licence expiry sets a hard end date on each game's sales life, and the delisting itself became a sales event for Horizon 4. For a licensed-car game, licences are a recurring cost and a time limit, not a one-off.

## 4. What do developers say about retention and reward pacing, and what criticisms exist?

### Takeaway
Playground's public position (Mike Brown, 2021) is that Game Pass design means lowering friction to return, releasing small regular batches of content and building social prompts. The main criticisms are that Horizon 4 and 5 gave rewards away too freely and that playlist-exclusive cars create fear of missing out; Horizon 6 responds to both, though some reviewers still find its progression thin.

## 5. Which lessons are transferable to a small independent drag racing game?

### Takeaway
The transferable parts are cheap ones: small scheduled content drops, a visible progression ladder, rewards that widen choice, a later route to any time-limited item, and never locking tuning behind per-car grinding. The parts that do not transfer are the licensed car list, the open world and Game Pass distribution.

### Cited Findings
- Small regular drops are the stated retention tool: "a snackable and manageable amount of stuff that's new" with more "in a few days" — [Press Start](https://press-start.com.au/news/xbox/2021/10/11/forza-horizon-5-was-built-entirely-with-xbox-game-pass-in-mind/)
- Low friction to return matters: "the friction to get you back in the game is very low" — [Press Start](https://press-start.com.au/news/xbox/2021/10/11/forza-horizon-5-was-built-entirely-with-xbox-game-pass-in-mind/)
- Over-generous early rewards were treated as a long-standing pacing flaw and corrected in Horizon 6 with rarer wheelspins and seven wristband tiers — [Mobalytics](https://mobalytics.gg/gamebase/reviews/forza-horizon-6-review); [Digital Citizen](https://www.digitalcitizen.life/forza-horizon-6-progression-explained-with-wristbands-stamps-and-horizon-play/)
- Per-car grinding to unlock upgrade parts was rejected by players and removed (all parts at Car Level 1 from Update 6) — [Top Gear](https://www.topgear.com/car-news/gaming/forza-motorsports-latest-update-changes-how-car-progression-works)
- Time-limited exclusive cars drew sustained complaints; Horizon 6 commits to bringing them back — [Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/forza/everything-we-know-about-the-forza-horizon-6-festival-playlist-so-far)
- Tradeable economies get exploited and need caps and fixes (2022 auction house closure; July 2026 cap removal) — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/forza-horizon-5-auction-house-closed-after-players-managed-to-trash-the-games-economy); [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/gaming/feature/forza-horizon-6s-latest-update-just-crashed-its-economy/)
- Real car licences expire and force delisting after roughly four to six years — [Forza Support FAQ](https://support.forza.net/hc/en-us/articles/30662484986387-Forza-Horizon-4-Delisting-FAQ); [Traxion](https://traxion.gg/farewell-forza-horizon-4-ye-shall-be-missed/)
- Discounts sharply raise unit sales late in a game's life: a 50% sale gave a 3.4x daily uplift on PS5 (estimate) — [Alinea Analytics](https://alineaanalytics.substack.com/p/forza-horizon-5-races-past-5m-copies)
- Old time-limited content can be re-run on a monthly rotation with no new development, as Turn 10 now does for Motorsport — [TheSixthAxis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2025/12/17/turn-off-the-engine-and-retire-the-car-turn-10-signals-end-of-forza-motorsport-support/)
- The mass audience for car games is casual and overlaps with mainstream titles more than with simulators (24% overlap with Gran Turismo 7 versus 89% with GTA 5) — [Alinea Analytics](https://alineaanalytics.substack.com/p/forza-horizon-5-races-past-5m-copies)

### Inferences
- Tuning is the core of drag racing, so the Motorsport lesson applies directly: let players buy and fit any part on a car they own from the start, and gate progress by money, class ladders or opponents instead of per-car experience.
- A drag game can copy the playlist cheaply: a weekly set of three to five bracket or class challenges with a small reward, built from existing cars and tracks, plus a rotating archive so nothing is permanently missed.
- A ladder like wristbands maps naturally to drag classes (for example by elapsed-time bracket), giving a visible long-term goal without an open world.
- Fictional or lookalike cars avoid licence fees, manufacturer content restrictions and forced delisting, which a small studio cannot absorb.
- If trading or an auction feature is considered, price floors and caps and exploit monitoring are needed from day one; a small team may be better served by sharing tunes and liveries only.
- A base price with planned seasonal discounts, and an optional supporter or content pack, is the scaled-down equivalent of Forza's edition ladder; paid early access attracts criticism and is unlikely to suit an indie launch.

### Gaps
- No evidence was found on how these mechanics perform at indie scale; all data comes from a franchise with tens of millions of players and subscription distribution.
- No Forza-specific data on drag racing modes or their popularity was found.
